Legal and privacy information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 · Free Windows release · v1.0

This policy concerns the current free release of Car Service Archiver, a Windows desktop application, and this information website. KupferSoft is a software brand used by an independent developer, not a representation of a separately incorporated company. For support and data inquiries, email carservicearchiver.support@gmail.com.

1. Records stored by the application

Customer names and contact details, vehicles and registration plates, service history, dates, mileage, work and parts, notes and photographs entered by users may include personal information. The application is designed to retain these records in a user-selected local folder using SQLite and associated media files. Some language, theme and setup preferences may also be saved on the device.

2. Automatic transmission to the developer

The reviewed current v35 source design does not provide a feature that automatically uploads customer databases or service photographs to the developer. The production application is designed for local use, with network requests restricted. This is not a guarantee concerning every operating system service, store service or third-party component.

3. User-initiated file operations

Backup, restore, CSV export and PDF/printing features can write to locations selected by the user. Saving files to a cloud-synced folder or sharing them with third parties is separate from the application's local storage design. Deleting a record or using the Trash function does not automatically erase previously made backups.

4. Support and feedback messages

The feedback feature opens the user's own email program; the user decides whether to send the email. Databases and service photographs are not attached automatically. If you contact support, your email address, subject, message, files you choose to attach, and correspondence may be processed to respond to and manage your request.

The support mailbox uses Google Gmail. Google's handling of email and technical data is governed by its own terms and privacy practices. Correspondence may be retained for as long as needed to manage the request and applicable legal obligations; the precise retention policy and processing grounds should be finalized by the developer before publication.

5. Website hosting and third parties

This information site is prepared for GitHub Pages. The pages contain no developer-installed analytics scripts, ad trackers or cookie-setting scripts. GitHub may nevertheless process IP addresses, browser details and request logs under its privacy statement. Download and acquisition data handled by Microsoft Store are governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.

6. Roles and responsibilities

Auto service businesses that choose why they collect customer information and how long it is stored must assess their own privacy and security obligations. Merely providing software does not automatically make the developer the controller of every customer's service records. Information voluntarily sent to developer support is a separate processing activity.

7. Access and deletion requests

For access, correction or deletion inquiries about correspondence sent to support, email carservicearchiver.support@gmail.com. Requests will be assessed under applicable law. Questions about records retained by an auto service business should normally be directed to that business.

8. Security and changes

Protect your chosen data folder, device account and backups against unauthorized access. This notice may be revised as application functionality or data practices change; the revision date is shown above.
