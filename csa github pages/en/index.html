Your records stay on your device

The application is designed to store customer and service records in a local folder you select. Device security and protecting backups remain important. Support emails reach the developer only if you choose to send them.

Frequently asked questions

Is Car Service Archiver free?

Yes. The first Windows release is planned to be free on Microsoft Store. The download link will be added after publication.

Where are the records stored?

Customer, vehicle and service records are designed to stay in a local folder you choose. You can also manage backups.

Need assistance?

Contact support with a bug report or feedback. Avoid attaching customer records or other personal data unless strictly necessary.
